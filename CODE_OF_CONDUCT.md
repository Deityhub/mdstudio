# Code of conduct

We want Markdown Studio to be a welcoming project. Everyone taking part is expected to:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the work, free of harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions, and block people who break these rules.
To report a problem, use **Security → Report a vulnerability** on this repository (it is private to
the maintainers) and say it is a conduct report.

This is a short adaptation of the principles in the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
