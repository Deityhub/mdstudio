## 1. Landing `/` — `01_home_page.png`, `08_home_checker_demo.png`

Header (`SiteHeader`, shared by the public pages, content up to 1200px wide): the `Logo` (two-pages mark plus the **md**studio wordmark, linking home), the links Features (`/#features`) and Guides (marked while on the guides), then a Settings icon and **Open the editor** (no search, theme or repository links here). - **Live checker demo** (`#try-it`): the real portability engine, lazy-loaded behind `ClientOnly`. An editable sample, four target pills with live issue counts, and a clickable report that selects the offending line.
- **Features** (six cards), **target profiles** (four cards), **About** and **Who it's for**, an eight-question **FAQ** (native `details`, mirrored in FAQPage JSON-LD), a closing call to action, and the footer (`SiteFooter`, in two groups: "mdstudio · version · MIT" on the left; Guides, Privacy, Terms and Changelog on the right).
- **Theme:** an inline head script applies the saved or OS theme before first paint; the theme button also lives in the studio header. ## 1c. Sharing from the editor — `14_share_dialog.png`, `15_comments_panel.png`

For a draft in the cloud (verified accounts only), the draft's menu has **Share** and the header has a **Share** button (an icon below 1024px); both open the "Share this draft" choice described in the top bar, and its **Share for review** leads to the dialog below. A small globe next to the cloud icon marks drafts that have a live link (a lock when only named people can open it). The Share dialog (`ShareDialog.tsx`) has two states:

- **Not shared:** "Who can open it" (Anyone with the link, or Only people I invite, which is off where the server turns private links off), a "Let reviewers comment" switch (both are chosen before publishing, so a private link is never public first), a "Link lasts" choice (1 to 7 days, 4 by default), a note that the page shows the text as it is in the cloud when published and that later edits stay private until an update, a note about images stored only on this device when the draft has any, and **Publish**.
- **Shared:** the link ("Public link" or "Private link") in a read-only field with **Copy**, "Who can open it" and "Let reviewers comment" (a change is saved at once), for a private link a **People who can open it** list (invite by email with **Invite**, up to 10, remove with the ✕ beside an address; adding an address emails it once), the publish and expiry dates, "Link lasts" (changing it sets the expiry that many days from now), a warning "Your draft has changes that are not published" when the draft differs from the published copy, **Publish update** (enabled only then) and **Stop sharing**. Publishing saves the draft and runs a sync first, so the copy is the latest text. Buttons show the loading state while a request runs; errors (not in the cloud yet, too many changes, offline, a full or unusable invite) appear in the dialog.

**Editing together.** **Edit together** is the second choice of **Share** (`CollabDialog.tsx`). Before it is on, the dialog says what it does and has **Start editing together** (it saves the draft and runs a sync first, so the room starts from the latest text); where the server or the account does not allow it, it says so. Once on, it has **People who can edit** (invite by email with **Invite**, up to the account's limit counting the owner, remove with the ✕ beside an address) and **Stop editing together** (the draft keeps its latest text, everyone invited is let go). While it is on, the dialog also shows the **Draft link** (read-only, with **Copy**) that the invitation emails carry. A draft being edited together has a people icon in the list. The footer of such a draft (`CollabStatus.tsx`) says **Live**, **Offline: your edits are kept here and will merge** or **Opening the shared draft…**, with a small avatar for each other person in it (their cursor colour, name on hover).

The invitation email's **Open the draft** button leads to `/c/<address>`: a page that asks for a sign-in if needed (with the invited address, creating an account is offered), then opens the draft; anyone not invited sees "You can't open this draft". Invitations and removals show up in **Shared with you** at once, without a reload. An invited person finds the draft under **Shared with you** at the bottom of their library (read when the studio opens and when the window returns; it shows who owns each draft). Opening one makes a local copy and opens it in the live editor; **Leave** (the exit icon, with a confirmation) removes it. These drafts are not in the person's own list, do not count against their cloud limit, are never uploaded, and leave the device at sign-out with what the browser kept of them. When the owner removes the person, stops editing together or deletes the draft, the open draft says so ("You can no longer edit …", with a line that changes made while offline stay on this device only; while the studio checks, because the room keeps turning the browser away, the footer says "Can't reach the room. Checking your access…") and stays on the device as an ordinary draft with its latest text. In the admin dashboard, the Overview shows how many drafts are edited together and how many people are invited, and the Server tab has the switch **Editing together is open**.

**Comments in a draft edited together.** When a draft is live, the **Comments** panel (also for people it is shared with) has a **Team** tab, next to **Reviewers** (the comments on a published link, when the draft has one). Select words and use **Comment** or **Suggest** (small buttons next to the AI button, or **Comment on selection** and **Suggest an edit** at the top of the panel, which also work on a phone). A comment opens a box for the text; a suggestion asks what to replace the words with (empty to suggest deleting them) and an optional reason. The words are marked in the editor (yellow for a comment, the accent colour for a suggestion) and move with the text; clicking them opens the thread. A thread shows the author, time, the words (**edited since** or **deleted** when they changed), the suggested change as a strike-through and its replacement, the replies, and a reply box. **Resolve** and **Reopen**, **Accept** and **Reject** for a suggestion (accepting makes the edit, which the accepter's Undo takes back; it says why when the words changed), and **Delete** for your own (the owner can delete any). Closed threads are behind **Show N closed**. When the owner stops editing together, the dialog says that comments end with it and that the open ones are kept in the draft's History.

**Images in a draft edited together.** An image added from a computer stays in that browser, so the others would see a broken picture. The one who added it gets a warning ("Only you can see this image") and the footer shows **N images only you can see**; it opens a list with advice (use an image address, a link to the picture) and, where hosting is on, **Host these images**. The others see **N images aren't shared with you** in the footer, with advice to ask for them to be hosted, and the preview shows such an image as a dashed box instead of a broken picture. The notes go away once the images are hosted or replaced by addresses.

A draft in the cloud also gets a **Comments** panel (speech-bubble button beside History; a bottom sheet on phones). Nothing is requested in the background: the link list is read when the Share dialog is opened (and the Comments panel needs it), and the comments are read when the panel is opened and after the writer changes one. It lists what reviewers wrote, open ones first, each with the author's display name, the date, the line it is about (**Line N: the words selected**, click to jump there), the text, a note when it was written on an earlier published copy, and, aligned to the right, **Resolve** / **Reopen** and **Delete** with its icon (replies sit under their comment). When there are none it says so, or that comments are off for this link.

## 1d. Shared page `/s/$slug` — `16_shared_page_comments.png`, `17_shared_page_phone.png`, `18_private_link_gate.png`

A public, read-only page for one published copy of a draft. Its own top bar (logo, a "Shared for review" or "Shared copy" chip, the theme button and **Open the editor**), the text and the footer all in one width: wide enough for the comments column while it shows beside the text with something in it, and just the text, centred, when the comments are hidden, there are none yet, or they sit below it (the bar and the footer narrow with it; the page starts narrow and widens when the first comment or notice appears), a meta line ("A copy shared with mdstudio on <date>. Available until <date>."), the text in `.md-preview` (readable at 360px with no sideways scroll; the comments sit in a column beside it when the writer takes them and the window is wide, below it on narrow screens), "Made with mdstudio · Report this page" under the text, and the site footer. Math and Mermaid diagrams are drawn in the browser after load, in the colors of the theme in use. An unknown, expired or revoked link is the site's 404 page.

**Private links.** A signed-out reader of a link limited to named people sees a standalone screen (logo and **Open the editor** above, a centred box): a "Private review link" chip, "This document is private" and a **Sign in** button, and none of the text; the page is `noindex`, `no-store`. The small sign-in dialog (`ReaderSignIn`: sign in or create an account, with a show/hide password button and the sign-up CAPTCHA when the server has one) leaves them on the page, which reloads for them. Someone signed in who was not invited gets the 404 page. An open private page adds "Only the people the writer named can open this."

**Comments** (when the writer turned them on), as in a document review. A reader selects words on the page and a **Comment** button appears beside the selection; pressing it opens a box in the column beside the page (a bottom sheet under 1024px) with the selected words quoted. The words stay highlighted, and each comment card sits level with its words, pushed down so cards never overlap. Clicking a highlight shows its comment and clicking a card scrolls to its words. **Hide comments** (a floating button, or the ✕ on the sheet) hides the column and **Comments (n)** shows it again, as does a click on any highlight. A signed-out reader is told "The writer is taking comments" with **Sign in to read and write comments**; a signed-in reader whose email is not confirmed is asked to confirm it. Cards show the author's name, the date, an "Earlier version" tag for comments written on an older copy, a "Resolved" tag, **Reply**, and **Delete** (with a confirmation) on the reader's own comments; while one is being deleted it is dimmed and its "Deleting" button shows a spinner. A comment whose words or block are gone from the current copy stays in the column, marked as being on an earlier version, without a highlight. Text is shown as plain text. The locked private-link page keeps the footer at the bottom of the window.

## 1e. Publishing to GitHub — `19_publish_connect.png`, `20_publish_github.png`

For a draft in the cloud, on a server that can publish (`githubPublish` in `/api/auth/me`), the draft's menu has **Publish** and the header's **Export** button becomes **Publish** (its menu starts with Publish to GitHub). The dialog (`PublishDialog.tsx`) reads from the server only when it opens or a choice needs it: the connection, what was published before, and the repositories. Nothing is requested in the background.

- **Not connected:** an explanation, **Also allow private repositories** (off by default: only public repositories and gists are reachable), a note that an organization may need to approve mdstudio, and **Connect GitHub**. It leaves for GitHub and returns to the editor, which reopens this dialog on the same draft.
- **Connected:** "Connected as <login>", **Allow private repositories** (when they were not allowed) and **Disconnect**, then two tabs.
  - **Repository:** a repository field (suggestions from the writer's repositories, or type any `owner/name`; picking or leaving the field reads its branches), **Branch** (the default one selected), **File path** (the title as a file name), **How** (commit to the branch, or open a pull request), an optional message, a note about how many local images will be added in an `images` folder beside the file, what was last published (date, repository, path) and "Your draft has changes that are not published", and the checks the GitHub profile finds (up to three lines; a local image the draft holds is not a problem). The button reads **Publish**, **Publish update** (same repository and path as last time) or **Open pull request**.
  - **Gist:** file name, description and **Public gist** (a secret gist by default; fixed once the gist exists, which is then updated in place: **Update gist**). Images are not included.
- **A file already there:** if the path holds a file this draft did not put there, or one changed on GitHub since, publishing stops with "<path> is already in <repo> on <branch>" and **Replace it**.
- **Done:** "Published" with links to the file (or the pull request, or the gist) and the commit, and **Done**.
- Errors appear in the dialog: not connected any more (it shows Connect again), repository or branch not found (with the private and organization hints), no write access, the branch moved meanwhile, images too large, GitHub unavailable, too many publishes.

## 2. Studio desktop `/editor/$docId` — `02_editor_split_view.png`, `13_library.png`, `11_export_menu.png`

**First visit and empty library.** `/editor` opens the first draft on the machine (or in the signed-in account). With none, it opens `/editor/new`, held in memory only and saved, under its own address, when the writer first types. On a first visit that is the example text; once this browser has ever stored a draft (a flag kept beside the drafts), it is a completely blank editor, so deleting the last draft, or reloading after it, never brings the example back. Until something is typed nothing is stored, so signing in with drafts in the cloud replaces either with those drafts. "New draft" always starts empty.

**Buttons.** Every enabled button, tab, menu item and switch shows a pointer cursor (a base rule in `src/styles.css`). `Button` has two busy states: `pending` (disabled, `aria-busy`) and `loading` (the same plus a spinner, which replaces the icon on an icon button). A button that starts a request shows it: the button that was pressed uses `loading` (disabled, with a spinner; on an icon button the spinner replaces the icon) for as long as its action runs, whether it is the main action of a form or dialog or a small action in a row (sign-in and sign-up, the reset links, the confirmation email, the cloud picker, publishing, saving a plan, account, limits or server settings, testing an AI connection, refreshing models, opening a generated draft, the account search, unlinking a sign-in, disconnecting GitHub, deleting a hosted image, resolving or deleting a comment, removing a person, saving or restoring a version, and the two-press admin buttons). The other buttons of the same dialog use `pending` (disabled, no spinner) while it runs, so nothing can be pressed twice. `disabled` alone is for a button that cannot be used yet (an empty field, a limit reached), never for one that is working; a test (`loading-rules.test.ts`) fails on a button disabled by a busy state without `loading` or `pending`, and on a hand-made spinner. The label of a button, menu item or command never ends in an ellipsis ("Edit rules", not "Edit rules…"); the ellipsis is kept only for text that says something is in progress ("Loading…", "Saving…"). Every dropdown choice uses the shared `Select` component (the target platform in the top bar and in Settings, a rule's severity, the AI provider and integration), never a native `<select>`.

- **Top bar:** one row from 640px (it wraps onto a second row only when the window is too narrow for everything). Left to right: drafts toggle (phones) · wordmark link with the release (`v0.2.0`, from package.json; hidden below 640px) and current title · target-profile Select (GFM extended, Strict CommonMark, GFM without HTML, Wiki notes; remembered per device) · view switch (Editor, Split, Preview as three icon buttons; Split hidden on phones) · panel buttons (768 to 1279px only) · Install (only when the browser offers to install the app) · AI writer · Import dropdown (files or a ZIP, or a folder) · **Export** · **Share**. On a phone the Select and the view switch form a second row.
- **Export / Publish:** one button. For a draft that is not in the cloud (or on a server that cannot publish) it is **Export**; for a cloud draft on a server that can publish to GitHub it is **Publish**, and its menu starts with a **Publish** section (Publish to GitHub) above the same Markdown, HTML and All drafts sections and the "Normalize on export" switch. Labels show from 1024px; below that only the icons.
- **Share:** one button, for cloud drafts only. It opens a "Share this draft" dialog with two choices: **Share for review** (the Share dialog below: a link readers can comment on) and **Edit together** (the invitation dialog below). The drafts menu keeps both as separate items.
- **Account:** the avatar (signed in: the menu with "Choose drafts for the cloud", "Manage images", Account, Settings, Admin dashboard and "Sign out"; signed out: Sign in and create an account, with "Forgot your password?" when the server sends mail and a `/reset-password` page for the emailed link; optional; "Continue with Google" and "Continue with GitHub" when the server has them) sits at the foot of the side strip from 768px, and at the end of the top bar on phones. The drafts list shows "Cloud: n of 5 drafts", a cloud icon on drafts stored in the account, and "Save to cloud" / "Remove from cloud" in each draft's menu.
- **Top bar, row 2:** target-profile Select (GFM extended, Strict CommonMark, GFM without HTML, Wiki notes; remembered per device) · view toggle (Editor / Split / Preview) · panel buttons on mid-size screens.
- **Export dropdown:** Download .md, Copy Markdown, Download self-contained .md and Copy self-contained Markdown (both enabled only when the draft has local images), Copy rendered HTML, Download standalone .html, and the "Normalize on export" switch.
- **Drafts sidebar:** New folder and New draft buttons, a search box, tag chips, and a folder tree. Search matches every word in a draft's title or text (a matching line is shown under the title); tag chips come from each draft's front matter `tags` and narrow the list when selected. Folders are labels kept in the library, not in the text: they list first (A to Z, collapsible, drafts indented beneath them with a guide line) and unfiled drafts follow. A draft moves by dragging it onto a folder, or onto the empty part of the list to take it out, and also from its three-dot menu (Rename, Move to folder, Save to cloud or Remove from cloud, then for a cloud draft Share, Publish (where the server can publish to GitHub) and Host images (where the server hosts them and the draft has local images), Duplicate, Delete; no item ends in an ellipsis), which is the route on touch screens. A folder's menu has Rename folder (an inline field with save and cancel; the drafts follow, and a name another folder already has is refused) and Delete folder: an empty one goes at once, one with drafts asks first because the drafts inside are deleted too. A folder also goes when its last draft is deleted (here or on another device); moving the last draft out leaves it. Delete opens a confirmation Dialog.
- **Formatting toolbar** (`role="toolbar"`, one tab stop, arrow keys / Home / End to move, Alt+F10 from the editor to enter it and Esc to leave; 17 buttons that wrap onto an extra row when the space runs out, never scroll: one row from 768px up (36px buttons), two rows on phones (40px touch targets)):
  undo, redo, **focus mode** (hides the left pane; `aria-pressed` while on; the drafts button in the top bar then opens the list as a panel from the left, closed by choosing a draft or Esc, with the cursor returned to the editor; the panel buttons stay too and open their panel from the left the same way, and the pane comes back as it was when focus mode ends; on phones the bottom panel bar stays as well) · heading style menu (Paragraph, H1-H6), bold, italic, strikethrough, inline code · link, image · quote, bulleted, numbered and task lists · code block menu, table menu, **Insert** menu.
  - Toggle tools show their state at the cursor (`aria-pressed`), and pressing one twice undoes it.
  - **Link** (Mod-K) and **Image** open dialogs. The link dialog edits the link under the caret; the image dialog requires alt text and accepts a URL or an uploaded file (stored in the draft).
  - **Code block** menu: fences the selection or current line with a language, switches the language inside a block, or removes the fence.
  - **Table** menu: Insert table (rows and columns dialog), and, inside a table, add or delete rows and columns, column alignment and Format table. Tab / Shift-Tab move between cells and Tab past the last cell adds a row.
  - **Insert** menu: Outdent / Indent · Footnote, Horizontal rule, Highlight, Inline math, Math block · Alert (Note, Tip, Important, Warning, Caution) · Diagram (Mermaid starters: flowchart, sequence, class, state, entity relationship, Gantt, pie) · HTML (collapsible section, keyboard key, subscript, superscript) · Keyboard shortcuts.
  - Every Insert-menu entry has an icon, and nested menus (Alert, Diagram, HTML) use icons too.
  - **Profile warnings:** a tool the selected platform won't render keeps working but carries a warning dot (buttons) or icon (menu items) and a hint such as "Supported in GFM extended and Wiki notes only". In menus the hint appears instantly on hover or keyboard focus, and the warning icon sits next to the submenu arrow. Raw HTML reads "may be removed or shown differently on other platforms": HTML is only partly supported (GitHub keeps `<details>`, `<kbd>`, `<sub>` and `<sup>`, for example) and each platform filters it differently. The same table drives the Checks panel, so the two never disagree.
  - Math and Mermaid tools insert the syntax, and the preview renders it for the GFM extended and Wiki notes profiles (see Preview below).
- **Editor** (CodeMirror, line numbers) and **Preview** (`.md-preview`) with two-way scroll sync (by source line, so both panes show the same text) and click-through sync: the block that holds the editor cursor gets a faint highlight in the preview, and clicking a block in the preview puts the editor cursor on its first source line (links, controls and text selections are left alone; from the preview-only view the click brings the editor back). The studio is a fixed shell: only the editor, preview and side panels scroll, never the page.
  - **Highlights:** `==text==` renders as a marker highlight for the Wiki notes profile; other profiles show the text unchanged.
  - **Smooth updates:** the preview replaces only the blocks that changed, so images, math and diagrams don't flash while you type; a diagram being edited keeps its previous version on screen until the new one is ready.
  - **Math:** `$x$`, `$$…$$` and ` ```math ` render with KaTeX for GFM extended and Wiki notes; `$5 and $10` stays text. KaTeX loads the first time a document needs it.
  - **Diagrams:** ` ```mermaid ` blocks render as SVG for GFM extended and Wiki notes; an invalid diagram keeps its code and shows the error beneath it. Other profiles show the source, as their platforms do.
  - **Export:** "Copy rendered HTML" and "Download standalone .html" include the rendered math (the standalone file embeds the KaTeX stylesheet and fonts) and inline SVG diagrams.
- **Side panel buttons:** Checks (badge with the count of warnings and errors), Outline, Front matter, History (saved versions of the draft; opening one shows a side-by-side comparison with the current text and a Restore button).
- **Status bar:** words · reading time · image count · an "Offline" note when there is no connection · sync status when signed in (Syncing… / Synced / Offline, will sync / Sync paused) · save status (Saving… / Saved in this browser / Not saved) · target platform.

## 6. AI writer dialog — `05_ai_writer_dialog.png`

- **Picker (shared by every AI task):** an AI integration Select, a Model Select, a Refresh models button and a Manage button. With no integration set up it shows "Set up an integration", which opens the AI integrations dialog on top; saving returns to the task with the new integration selected. An integration that lost its key after a reload shows "needs its API key again" with an "Add key" button.
- **Where integrations are kept:** signed out, in this browser; signed in (with a confirmed email), in the account, so they follow the writer to other devices. The dialog says which. Signing in moves this browser's own integrations into the account. An account keeps up to 20 (the Add button is disabled with a note at the limit). Keys are never kept: an integration from another device shows "needs its API key again".
- **AI integrations dialog:** a list (name, provider, Ready or Key needed, edit, remove) and an add/edit form: provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Ollama on this computer, custom/OpenAI-compatible), optional name, base URL (Ollama and custom), API key (not for Ollama) and model IDs (custom: required and the only choices; Ollama: optional). Also reached from the AI menu as "AI integrations". Keys stay in memory; the rest is remembered on this device.
- **Fix with AI:** in the Checks panel a fixable issue has a "Fix with AI" button ("Suggest alt text" for missing alt). It opens the same dialog as the inline actions, with the picker, Run, a diff and Accept.
- **Integration form extras:** "Test connection" (reports the number of models found, or the provider's error) and, for custom endpoints, preset buttons that fill the name and base URL.
- **Layout:** the title, picker and tabs stay at the top and the action buttons stay at the bottom while the middle scrolls, and it follows the streamed text unless the reader scrolls up. Applying a suggestion or opening a generated draft closes the dialog and clears the result.
- **Inline actions:** the toolbar has an AI menu (sparkles) and, from 768px up, a small AI button appears over selected text. Both list the actions; an action opens a dialog that streams the result and shows a before/after diff with Accept, Try again and Discard. Translate asks for a language and Custom for an instruction. With nothing selected a toast asks for a selection.
- **Tab "Title, description & tags":** the current draft (editable, or uploaded) → "Suggest metadata" → editable suggested fields → apply to the draft's front matter.
- **Tab "Write a topic":** topic and notes → "Generate draft" streams Markdown → Stop / Open as new draft (never overwrites the current draft).
- Errors are shown inline (auth, quota, network/CORS).

## 6a. Admin dashboard: `/admin`

Only for an admin; anyone else sees the site's 404. Reached from "Admin dashboard" in the account menu. The app frame (`AppShell`): a top bar (on the page background, like the public header) with the logo, an "Admin" chip, a primary "Back to the editor" button and the account menu (its contents line up with the page's content below it), and a menu of sections on the left (a row of chips on phones; kept in the address, so a link opens one), each section with its title and a line under it: **Overview** (cards of site totals), **Accounts** (a search box, a table of accounts with plan, cloud drafts, status badges and join date; opening one shows a dialog with Account controls (role, plan, suspended, email confirmed, Save account, Sign out everywhere, Delete account, each destructive one asking for a second press), Usage, "Limits for this account" (a number field or switch per limit, grouped as Storage and counts, Sharing and publishing, Rate limits, each saying where its value comes from, with Reset and Save limits) and Content (cloud drafts, share links and images by title and size, each with a two-press Remove)), **Plans** (list, New plan form with name, label and the same limit controls), **Server** (a switch for new accounts, a site notice, and default limits for every account), **Audit log** (who did what to whom, newest first). The site notice, when set, shows as a banner above the editor.

## 6b. Settings: `/settings`

Opened from the Account menu (**Account** opens the Account tab, **Settings** the Editor tab), from the gear in the editor's side strip (and in its top bar on phones), and from the gear in the header of the public pages. The app frame (`AppShell`, as the dashboard): a top bar (on the page background, like the public header) with the logo, "/ Settings", a primary "Back to the editor" button and the account menu (its contents line up with the page's content below it), and the sections in a menu on the left (a row of chips on phones), with the open one in the address (`?tab=`). Each section has its title and a line under it, and its rows are grouped in bordered cards: **Account** (signed in only: picture, name and address; Display name, Email and Password rows, each with an Edit button that opens a dialog: the name field, the new address with the current password (a link is emailed to it and the address changes when it is opened; disabled for an account with no password, which sets one first), or the current and the new password (an account that signs in with GitHub or Google has no current one and sees Set password); changing the password signs the other devices out), then **Sign in with GitHub** and **Sign in with Google** rows (when the server has them) with Link, which leaves for the provider and comes back with a toast, or Unlink, disabled with an explanation while it is the only way to sign in). The profile sits in its own card, the sign-ins under a "Sign-in methods" heading., **Editor** (theme as a System | Light | Dark switch, target platform, Normalize on export, a button for the writing-check rules and one for keyboard shortcuts; all kept on this device), **AI** (the integrations listed in the card, each with its provider, Ready or Key needed, an Update button that opens the dialog on its form and a Remove button; an Add integration button opens the dialog on a blank form; the dialog closes after saving) and **Connections** (cloud drafts, hosted images when the server has them, and GitHub publishing with Connect or Disconnect; signed in only). A signed-out visitor sees only Editor and AI and cannot reach the others by address. The rules, shortcuts, AI, cloud-drafts and images dialogs are the same ones the editor opens, so they stay where they are used mid-task. noindex.

## 6b-2. Standalone screens and 404

The screens that stand on their own (the private-link gate, `/c/<address>` for an invitation, `/reset-password`, any address that leads nowhere, and a page that failed to load) share one frame (`Standalone`): a bar with the logo and **Open the editor**, and the content centred below in a bordered box. ## 6c. Search palette

A dialog for finding anything, on every page: Ctrl/⌘ Shift P anywhere, Ctrl/⌘ K anywhere except in the editor's text (where it stays Link), or the Search button in the editor's top bar, the Settings header and the public pages' header (not on the home page, which keeps its header for the call to open the editor; the shortcuts work there). One field and grouped results: **Drafts** (by name, title or folder), **In drafts** (drafts whose text holds every word, with the matching line; choosing one opens the draft at that line), **Headings** of the open draft, **Commands** (in the editor: new draft, import, the exports, views, panels, focus, insert link, image or table, save a version, the rules, AI and shortcuts dialogs, and share, edit together and publish for a cloud draft; everywhere: theme), **Settings** (each row of the Settings page, opening its tab) and **Pages** (editor, settings, guides and each guide, privacy, terms, and the admin dashboard for an admin). Arrow keys move, Enter opens, Esc closes; with nothing typed it lists the recent drafts and the first commands. Its code loads the first time it opens.

## 7. Small screens — `06_mobile_editor_view.png`, `07_mobile_preview_view.png`

- **Below 768px** the studio is a single pane: the view toggle offers Editor and Preview only, and the panel buttons move to a bottom navigation bar.
- **Below 1280px** the drafts list and Checks, Outline, Front matter, History and Comments open as a sheet from the left (from the side strip, or on phones from the top bar's drafts button and the bottom bar), sharing the same components as the docked pane.
- **From 768px** (everything but phones) a Drafts button and the panel buttons (Checks, Outline, Front matter, History, Comments) sit in a 48px column at the left edge, with Search, the theme button, Settings and the account at its foot. **At 1280px and above**, beside them, one docked pane that the drafts and the panels share: a panel takes the pane, pressing its button again closes it and gives the pane back to the drafts, and the Drafts button there shows or hides the list (the top bar's drafts toggle exists only on phones). There is no panel column on the right. The pane defaults to 288px and can be dragged (or resized with the arrow keys, Home and End; double-click resets) between 240px and 480px; the width is remembered. The drafts show first; Checks opens from its button.

## Interactions

- Autosave 400ms after typing. Unsaved edits are flushed immediately when switching drafts, leaving the studio, hiding the tab or closing the page. A failed save shows "Not saved" and one toast.
- Drag-drop `.md` / `.markdown` / `.mdown` / `.txt` files to import them as new drafts; dropping images compresses them and inserts `![name](./images/name.jpg)`.
- Pasting rich HTML converts it to Markdown, with a toast that explains how to undo.
- Shortcuts (also listed in the app: Insert menu > Keyboard shortcuts, or Mod-/): Mod-B bold, Mod-I italic, Mod-E inline code, Mod-Shift-X strikethrough, Mod-Shift-H highlight, Mod-K link, Mod-Alt-0..6 paragraph and headings, Mod-Shift-7 numbered list, Mod-Shift-8 bulleted list, Mod-Shift-L task list, Mod-Shift-. quote, Tab / Shift-Tab nest and outdent list items, Mod-F search, Mod-Z / Shift-Mod-Z undo and redo, Mod-/ shortcuts list, Alt-F10 focus the toolbar. Enter continues lists and quotes.

- **History panel, cloud drafts:** below the local versions, an "In your cloud" section lists the versions the server keeps (time, size, and "Replaced by a newer edit elsewhere" where one was taken over by another device). Opening one fetches its text into the same compare dialog, and restoring is an ordinary edit.
