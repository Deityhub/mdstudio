## 1. Landing `/` — `01_home_page.png`, `08_home_checker_demo.png`

Header: the `Logo` (two-pages mark plus the **md**studio wordmark, linking home), a theme button (each click goes Light, Dark, System and round again; its icon and label show the current choice) and a GitHub link (opens the repository in a new tab). Sections, top to bottom:

- **Theme:** an inline head script applies the saved or OS theme before first paint; the theme button also lives in the studio header. "System" forgets any saved choice and follows the OS, including when it switches while the page is open; Light and Dark are remembered on this device and ignore the OS.

## 1a. Privacy and Terms `/privacy`, `/terms`

Plain text pages in the site layout (header, footer), with the title and "Last updated" date, linked from the footer. Public and indexable.

## 1c. Sharing from the editor — `14_share_dialog.png`, `15_comments_panel.png`

For a draft in the cloud (verified accounts only), the draft's menu has **Share…** and the header has a **Share** button (an icon on small screens). A small globe next to the cloud icon marks drafts that have a live link (a lock when only named people can open it). The Share dialog (`ShareDialog.tsx`) has two states:

- **Not shared:** "Who can open it" (Anyone with the link, or Only people I invite, which is off where the server turns private links off), a "Let reviewers comment" switch (both are chosen before publishing, so a private link is never public first), a "Link lasts" choice (1 to 7 days, 4 by default), a note that the page shows the text as it is in the cloud when published and that later edits stay private until an update, a note about images stored only on this device when the draft has any, and **Publish**.
- **Shared:** the link ("Public link" or "Private link") in a read-only field with **Copy**, "Who can open it" and "Let reviewers comment" (a change is saved at once), for a private link a **People who can open it** list (invite by email with **Invite**, up to 10, remove with the ✕ beside an address; adding an address emails it once), the publish and expiry dates, "Link lasts" (changing it sets the expiry that many days from now), a warning "Your draft has changes that are not published" when the draft differs from the published copy, **Publish update** (enabled only then) and **Stop sharing**. Publishing saves the draft and runs a sync first, so the copy is the latest text. Buttons show the loading state while a request runs; errors (not in the cloud yet, too many changes, offline, a full or unusable invite) appear in the dialog.

**Editing together.** A cloud draft's menu also has **Edit together…** (`CollabDialog.tsx`). Before it is on, the dialog says what it does and has **Start editing together** (it saves the draft and runs a sync first, so the room starts from the latest text); where the server or the account does not allow it, it says so. Once on, it has **People who can edit** (invite by email with **Invite**, up to the account's limit counting the owner, remove with the ✕ beside an address) and **Stop editing together** (the draft keeps its latest text, everyone invited is let go). While it is on, the dialog also shows the **Draft link** (read-only, with **Copy**) that the invitation emails carry. A draft being edited together has a people icon in the list. The footer of such a draft (`CollabStatus.tsx`) says **Live**, **Offline: your edits are kept here and will merge** or **Opening the shared draft…**, with a small avatar for each other person in it (their cursor colour, name on hover).

The invitation email's **Open the draft** button leads to `/c/<address>`: a page that asks for a sign-in if needed (with the invited address, creating an account is offered), then opens the draft; anyone not invited sees "You can't open this draft". Invitations and removals show up in **Shared with you** at once, without a reload. An invited person finds the draft under **Shared with you** at the bottom of their library (read when the studio opens and when the window returns; it shows who owns each draft). Opening one makes a local copy and opens it in the live editor; **Leave** (the exit icon, with a confirmation) removes it. These drafts are not in the person's own list, do not count against their cloud limit, are never uploaded, and leave the device at sign-out with what the browser kept of them. When the owner removes the person, stops editing together or deletes the draft, the open draft says so ("You can no longer edit …", with a line that changes made while offline stay on this device only; while the studio checks, because the room keeps turning the browser away, the footer says "Can't reach the room. Checking your access…") and stays on the device as an ordinary draft with its latest text. In the admin dashboard, the Overview shows how many drafts are edited together and how many people are invited, and the Server tab has the switch **Editing together is open**.

**Comments in a draft edited together.** When a draft is live, the **Comments** panel (also for people it is shared with) has a **Team** tab, next to **Reviewers** (the comments on a published link, when the draft has one). Select words and use **Comment** or **Suggest** (small buttons next to the AI button, or **Comment on selection** and **Suggest an edit** at the top of the panel, which also work on a phone). A comment opens a box for the text; a suggestion asks what to replace the words with (empty to suggest deleting them) and an optional reason. The words are marked in the editor (yellow for a comment, the accent colour for a suggestion) and move with the text; clicking them opens the thread. A thread shows the author, time, the words (**edited since** or **deleted** when they changed), the suggested change as a strike-through and its replacement, the replies, and a reply box. **Resolve** and **Reopen**, **Accept** and **Reject** for a suggestion (accepting makes the edit, which the accepter's Undo takes back; it says why when the words changed), and **Delete** for your own (the owner can delete any). Closed threads are behind **Show N closed**. When the owner stops editing together, the dialog says that comments end with it and that the open ones are kept in the draft's History.

A draft in the cloud also gets a **Comments** panel (speech-bubble button beside History; a bottom sheet on phones). Nothing is requested in the background: the link list is read when the Share dialog is opened (and the Comments panel needs it), and the comments are read when the panel is opened and after the writer changes one. It lists what reviewers wrote, open ones first, each with the author's display name, the date, the line it is about (**Line N: the words selected**, click to jump there), the text, a note when it was written on an earlier published copy, and, aligned to the right, **Resolve** / **Reopen** and **Delete** with its icon (replies sit under their comment). When there are none it says so, or that comments are off for this link.

## 1d. Shared page `/s/$slug` — `16_shared_page_comments.png`, `17_shared_page_phone.png`, `18_private_link_gate.png`

A public, read-only page for one published copy of a draft. The site header, a one-line notice ("A copy shared with mdstudio on <date>. Available until <date>. Report this page"), the text in `.md-preview` (single column, up to `max-w-3xl`, readable at 360px with no sideways scroll) and the site footer. Math and Mermaid diagrams are drawn in the browser after load, in the colors of the theme in use. An unknown, expired or revoked link is the site's 404 page.

**Private links.** A signed-out reader of a link limited to named people sees a lock, "This document is private" and a **Sign in** button, and none of the text; the page is `noindex`, `no-store`. The small sign-in dialog (`ReaderSignIn`: sign in or create an account, with a show/hide password button and the sign-up CAPTCHA when the server has one) leaves them on the page, which reloads for them. Someone signed in who was not invited gets the 404 page. An open private page adds "Only the people the writer named can open this."

**Comments** (when the writer turned them on), as in a document review. A reader selects words on the page and a **Comment** button appears beside the selection; pressing it opens a box in the column beside the page (a bottom sheet under 1024px) with the selected words quoted. The words stay highlighted, and each comment card sits level with its words, pushed down so cards never overlap. Clicking a highlight shows its comment and clicking a card scrolls to its words. **Hide comments** (a floating button, or the ✕ on the sheet) hides the column and **Comments (n)** shows it again, as does a click on any highlight. A signed-out reader is told "The writer is taking comments" with **Sign in to read and write comments**; a signed-in reader whose email is not confirmed is asked to confirm it. Cards show the author's name, the date, an "Earlier version" tag for comments written on an older copy, a "Resolved" tag, **Reply**, and **Delete** (with a confirmation) on the reader's own comments. A comment whose words or block are gone from the current copy stays in the column, marked as being on an earlier version, without a highlight. Text is shown as plain text. The locked private-link page keeps the footer at the bottom of the window.

## 1e. Publishing to GitHub — `19_publish_connect.png`, `20_publish_github.png`

For a draft in the cloud, on a server that can publish (`githubPublish` in `/api/auth/me`), the draft's menu has **Publish to GitHub…** and the header has a **Publish** button (the GitHub icon alone below the xl breakpoint). The dialog (`PublishDialog.tsx`) reads from the server only when it opens or a choice needs it: the connection, what was published before, and the repositories. Nothing is requested in the background.

- **Not connected:** an explanation, **Also allow private repositories** (off by default: only public repositories and gists are reachable), a note that an organization may need to approve mdstudio, and **Connect GitHub**. It leaves for GitHub and returns to the editor, which reopens this dialog on the same draft.
- **Connected:** "Connected as <login>", **Allow private repositories** (when they were not allowed) and **Disconnect**, then two tabs.
  - **Repository:** a repository field (suggestions from the writer's repositories, or type any `owner/name`; picking or leaving the field reads its branches), **Branch** (the default one selected), **File path** (the title as a file name), **How** (commit to the branch, or open a pull request), an optional message, a note about how many local images will be added in an `images` folder beside the file, what was last published (date, repository, path) and "Your draft has changes that are not published", and the checks the GitHub profile finds (up to three lines; a local image the draft holds is not a problem). The button reads **Publish**, **Publish update** (same repository and path as last time) or **Open pull request**.
  - **Gist:** file name, description and **Public gist** (a secret gist by default; fixed once the gist exists, which is then updated in place: **Update gist**). Images are not included.
- **A file already there:** if the path holds a file this draft did not put there, or one changed on GitHub since, publishing stops with "<path> is already in <repo> on <branch>" and **Replace it**.
- **Done:** "Published" with links to the file (or the pull request, or the gist) and the commit, and **Done**.
- Errors appear in the dialog: not connected any more (it shows Connect again), repository or branch not found (with the private and organization hints), no write access, the branch moved meanwhile, images too large, GitHub unavailable, too many publishes.

## 2. Studio desktop `/editor/$docId` — `02_editor_split_view.png`, `13_library.png`, `11_export_menu.png`

**First visit and empty library.** `/editor` opens the first draft on the machine (or in the signed-in account). With none, it opens `/editor/new`, held in memory only and saved, under its own address, when the writer first types. On a first visit that is the example text; once this browser has ever stored a draft (a flag kept beside the drafts), it is a completely blank editor, so deleting the last draft, or reloading after it, never brings the example back. Until something is typed nothing is stored, so signing in with drafts in the cloud replaces either with those drafts. "New draft" always starts empty.

**Buttons.** Every enabled button, tab, menu item and switch shows a pointer cursor (a base rule in `src/styles.css`). `Button` has two busy states: `pending` (disabled, `aria-busy`) and `loading` (the same plus a spinner, which replaces the icon on an icon button). Sign-in and sign-up, the reset links, the confirmation email and the cloud picker use `loading`.

- **Top bar, row 1:** drafts toggle · wordmark link with the release (`v0.2.0`, from package.json; hidden below 640px) and current title · Import dropdown (files or a ZIP, or a folder) · Install (only when the browser offers to install the app) · AI writer · Export dropdown (including Download all drafts as a ZIP) · Account (sign in or create an account, with "Forgot your password?" when the server sends mail and a `/reset-password` page for the emailed link; optional; "Continue with Google" and "Continue with GitHub" when the server has them). Signed in, the menu has "Choose drafts for the cloud…" and "Manage images…" (when this server hosts images) and "Sign out"; the drafts list shows "Cloud: n of 5 drafts", a cloud icon on drafts stored in the account, and "Save to cloud" / "Remove from cloud" in each draft's menu.
- **Top bar, row 2:** target-profile Select (GFM extended, Strict CommonMark, GFM without HTML, Wiki notes; remembered per device) · view toggle (Editor / Split / Preview) · panel buttons on mid-size screens.
- **Export dropdown:** Download .md, Copy Markdown, Download self-contained .md and Copy self-contained Markdown (both enabled only when the draft has local images), Copy rendered HTML, Download standalone .html, and the "Normalize on export" switch.
- **Drafts sidebar:** New folder and New draft buttons, a search box, tag chips, and a folder tree. Search matches every word in a draft's title or text (a matching line is shown under the title); tag chips come from each draft's front matter `tags` and narrow the list when selected. Folders are labels kept in the library, not in the text: they list first (A to Z, collapsible, drafts indented beneath them with a guide line) and unfiled drafts follow. A draft moves by dragging it onto a folder, or onto the empty part of the list to take it out, and also from its three-dot menu (Rename, Move to folder, Duplicate, Delete), which is the route on touch screens. A folder's menu has Rename folder (an inline field with save and cancel; the drafts follow, and a name another folder already has is refused) and Delete folder: an empty one goes at once, one with drafts asks first because the drafts inside are deleted too. Delete opens a confirmation Dialog.
- **Formatting toolbar** (`role="toolbar"`, one tab stop, arrow keys / Home / End to move, Alt+F10 from the editor to enter it and Esc to leave; 17 buttons that wrap onto an extra row when the space runs out, never scroll: one row from 768px up (36px buttons), two rows on phones (40px touch targets)):
  undo, redo, **focus mode** (hides the side panels; `aria-pressed` while on; the drafts button in the top bar then opens the list as a panel from the left, closed by choosing a draft or Esc, with the cursor returned to the editor; the right-hand panel buttons stay too and open their panel from the right the same way, while the panel docked beside the editor is hidden and comes back as it was when focus mode ends; on phones the bottom panel bar stays as well) · heading style menu (Paragraph, H1-H6), bold, italic, strikethrough, inline code · link, image · quote, bulleted, numbered and task lists · code block menu, table menu, **Insert** menu.
  - Toggle tools show their state at the cursor (`aria-pressed`), and pressing one twice undoes it.
  - **Link** (Mod-K) and **Image** open dialogs. The link dialog edits the link under the caret; the image dialog requires alt text and accepts a URL or an uploaded file (stored in the draft).
  - **Code block** menu: fences the selection or current line with a language, switches the language inside a block, or removes the fence.
  - **Table** menu: Insert table (rows and columns dialog), and, inside a table, add or delete rows and columns, column alignment and Format table. Tab / Shift-Tab move between cells and Tab past the last cell adds a row.
  - **Insert** menu: Outdent / Indent · Footnote, Horizontal rule, Highlight, Inline math, Math block · Alert (Note, Tip, Important, Warning, Caution) · Diagram (Mermaid starters: flowchart, sequence, class, state, entity relationship, Gantt, pie) · HTML (collapsible section, keyboard key, subscript, superscript) · Keyboard shortcuts.
  - Every Insert-menu entry has an icon, and nested menus (Alert, Diagram, HTML) use icons too.
  - **Profile warnings:** a tool the selected platform won't render keeps working but carries a warning dot (buttons) or icon (menu items) and a hint such as "Supported in GFM extended and Wiki notes only". In menus the hint appears instantly on hover or keyboard focus, and the warning icon sits next to the submenu arrow. Raw HTML reads "may be removed or shown differently on other platforms": HTML is only partly supported (GitHub keeps `<details>`, `<kbd>`, `<sub>` and `<sup>`, for example) and each platform filters it differently. The same table drives the Checks panel, so the two never disagree.
  - Math and Mermaid tools insert the syntax, and the preview renders it for the GFM extended and Wiki notes profiles (see Preview below).
- **Editor** (CodeMirror, line numbers) and **Preview** (`.md-preview`) with two-way scroll sync (by source line, so both panes show the same text) and click-through sync: the block that holds the editor cursor gets a faint highlight in the preview, and clicking a block in the preview puts the editor cursor on its first source line (links, controls and text selections are left alone; from the preview-only view the click brings the editor back). The studio is a fixed shell: only the editor, preview and side panels scroll, never the page.
  - **Highlights:** `==text==` renders as a marker highlight for the Wiki notes profile; other profiles show the text unchanged.
  - **Smooth updates:** the preview replaces only the blocks that changed, so images, math and diagrams don't flash while you type; a diagram being edited keeps its previous version on screen until the new one is ready.
  - **Math:** `$x$`, `$$…$$` and ` ```math ` render with KaTeX for GFM extended and Wiki notes; `$5 and $10` stays text. KaTeX loads the first time a document needs it.
  - **Diagrams:** ` ```mermaid ` blocks render as SVG for GFM extended and Wiki notes; an invalid diagram keeps its code and shows the error beneath it. Other profiles show the source, as their platforms do.
  - **Export:** "Copy rendered HTML" and "Download standalone .html" include the rendered math (the standalone file embeds the KaTeX stylesheet and fonts) and inline SVG diagrams.
- **Side panel buttons:** Checks (badge with the count of warnings and errors), Outline, Front matter, History (saved versions of the draft; opening one shows a side-by-side comparison with the current text and a Restore button).
- **Status bar:** words · reading time · image count · an "Offline" note when there is no connection · sync status when signed in (Syncing… / Synced / Offline, will sync / Sync paused) · save status (Saving… / Saved in this browser / Not saved) · target platform.

## 6. AI writer dialog — `05_ai_writer_dialog.png`

- **Picker (shared by every AI task):** an AI integration Select, a Model Select, a Refresh models button and a Manage button. With no integration set up it shows "Set up an integration", which opens the AI integrations dialog on top; saving returns to the task with the new integration selected. An integration that lost its key after a reload shows "needs its API key again" with an "Add key" button.
- **Where integrations are kept:** signed out, in this browser; signed in (with a confirmed email), in the account, so they follow the writer to other devices. The dialog says which. Signing in moves this browser's own integrations into the account. An account keeps up to 20 (the Add button is disabled with a note at the limit). Keys are never kept: an integration from another device shows "needs its API key again".
- **AI integrations dialog:** a list (name, provider, Ready or Key needed, edit, remove) and an add/edit form: provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, Ollama on this computer, custom/OpenAI-compatible), optional name, base URL (Ollama and custom), API key (not for Ollama) and model IDs (custom: required and the only choices; Ollama: optional). Also reached from the AI menu as "AI integrations…". Keys stay in memory; the rest is remembered on this device.
- **Fix with AI:** in the Checks panel a fixable issue has a "Fix with AI" button ("Suggest alt text" for missing alt). It opens the same dialog as the inline actions, with the picker, Run, a diff and Accept.
- **Integration form extras:** "Test connection" (reports the number of models found, or the provider's error) and, for custom endpoints, preset buttons that fill the name and base URL.
- **Layout:** the title, picker and tabs stay at the top and the action buttons stay at the bottom while the middle scrolls, and it follows the streamed text unless the reader scrolls up. Applying a suggestion or opening a generated draft closes the dialog and clears the result.
- **Inline actions:** the toolbar has an AI menu (sparkles) and, from 768px up, a small AI button appears over selected text. Both list the actions; an action opens a dialog that streams the result and shows a before/after diff with Accept, Try again and Discard. Translate asks for a language and Custom for an instruction. With nothing selected a toast asks for a selection.
- **Tab "Title, description & tags":** the current draft (editable, or uploaded) → "Suggest metadata" → editable suggested fields → apply to the draft's front matter.
- **Tab "Write a topic":** topic and notes → "Generate draft" streams Markdown → Stop / Open as new draft (never overwrites the current draft).
- Errors are shown inline (auth, quota, network/CORS).

## 7. Small screens — `06_mobile_editor_view.png`, `07_mobile_preview_view.png`

- **Below 768px** the studio is a single pane: the view toggle offers Editor and Preview only, and the panel buttons move to a bottom navigation bar.
- **Below 1280px** the drafts list opens as a left sheet and Checks, Outline and Front matter open as a right sheet, sharing the same panel components as the desktop sidebars.
- **At 1280px and above** the drafts sidebar and the panel column are docked. The drafts sidebar defaults to 288px and can be dragged (or resized with the arrow keys, Home and End; double-click resets) between 240px and 480px; the width is remembered. The Checks panel starts open only from 1536px; below that it stays closed until its button is clicked.

## Interactions

- Autosave 400ms after typing. Unsaved edits are flushed immediately when switching drafts, leaving the studio, hiding the tab or closing the page. A failed save shows "Not saved" and one toast.
- Drag-drop `.md` / `.markdown` / `.mdown` / `.txt` files to import them as new drafts; dropping images compresses them and inserts `![name](./images/name.jpg)`.
- Pasting rich HTML converts it to Markdown, with a toast that explains how to undo.
- Shortcuts (also listed in the app: Insert menu > Keyboard shortcuts, or Mod-/): Mod-B bold, Mod-I italic, Mod-E inline code, Mod-Shift-X strikethrough, Mod-Shift-H highlight, Mod-K link, Mod-Alt-0..6 paragraph and headings, Mod-Shift-7 numbered list, Mod-Shift-8 bulleted list, Mod-Shift-L task list, Mod-Shift-. quote, Tab / Shift-Tab nest and outdent list items, Mod-F search, Mod-Z / Shift-Mod-Z undo and redo, Mod-/ shortcuts list, Alt-F10 focus the toolbar. Enter continues lists and quotes.

- **History panel, cloud drafts:** below the local versions, an "In your cloud" section lists the versions the server keeps (time, size, and "Replaced by a newer edit elsewhere" where one was taken over by another device). Opening one fetches its text into the same compare dialog, and restoring is an ordinary edit.
